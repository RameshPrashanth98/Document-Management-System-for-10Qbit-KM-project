A software development project focused on supporting document organization and knowledge management for 10Qbit. The project aims to provide a structured approach to managing digital documents, making information easier to organize, access, and maintain within a knowledge management environment.

Project Objectives

Centralized Document Management: Support the organized management of digital documents and project-related information.

Knowledge Accessibility: Make relevant documentation easier to locate and access.

Information Organization: Improve how documents and knowledge resources are structured and maintained.

Operational Efficiency: Reduce the effort involved in managing and retrieving project documentation.

This project provided an opportunity to explore document management concepts, knowledge management workflows, and the development of software solutions for organizational information management.
